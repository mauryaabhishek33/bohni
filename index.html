The four seconds at the counter

A shopkeeper cannot watch a screen and serve a customer at the same time.

That is the whole problem. Everything the app does follows from it — and from the fact that a counter is loud, hands are busy, and the person paying is already walking away.

    The customer scans

    Money lands in the bank. The phone is in a drawer, or face-down beside the weighing scale, or charging on a nail in the wall.

  2. 02

What it does

Features, in the order a merchant meets them.

Soundbox

Every payment announced out loud

“One hundred rupees received, from Ramesh.” Loud enough to hear across a counter, in English, Hindi and ten more Indian languages — the spoken language set separately from the app’s own.

Khata

The ledger keeps itself

Read from the notifications UPI and bank apps already show, and from bank alert SMS if you allow it. Amount, who paid, reference, time. Nothing typed.

Cash

Add a cash sale in two taps

A shop’s takings are not only UPI. And any row can be corrected or deleted — a misread amount included.

Bills

Receipts and GST invoices

Made in seconds and shared as a PDF on WhatsApp. A simple bill if you are not registered, a tax invoice if you are — with the shop’s name, the number and the exact time on every one.

Shop, or your own money

Tracking your own money is free, always. There is nothing to buy and nothing that expires. Say which you are on the first screen and change it in Settings whenever — a shop gets announcements and bills, and pays ₹29 a month after the first six months.

Insight

Takings, customers, UPI charges

What came in this week, who your best customers are, and how close you are to the point where UPI stops being free for your shop.

Yours to take

Your whole ledger, or a full backup file, whenever you like and free in every case — including after the trial ends. Your records are never held hostage.

Yours to bring

Import what you already have

A backup from your old phone, or a plain spreadsheet you kept yourself. Excel and CSV both, in and out, so nothing is trapped in here.

On this phone

Stored only on this phone

Your payments are read on this phone and stored only on this phone — not even to Google backup. No account, no server, nothing to log in to. No amount, sender or notification text is ever uploaded, shared or sold.

UPI charges

Know before UPI starts costing you

Bohni adds every payment up as it arrives and warns you at four-fifths of the monthly limit — in time to take the rest of the month in cash, rather than finding out from your bank three months later.

One ledger

Every UPI app in the same book

It does not matter which app the money moved through, or whether you paid or were paid. Each one lands in the same ledger, in order, added up — so the total is the whole picture and not one app’s share of it.

UPI charges

The number that decides whether UPI is free for your shop.

From 15 October 2026 a small merchant keeps zero MDR only while money in stays at or under ₹1,00,000 a month, and a bank only reclassifies after three consecutive months over. So the month that costs you is the third one, and by then it is too late to do anything about it.

Bohni counts the month as it happens and tells you where you stand, in time to take the rest of the month in cash if you want to.

Your category is your bank’s classification and no app can read it, so Bohni works from what you tell it and says so on the screen.

Your money, your phone

Payments are read on this phone and stored only on this phone — not even to Google backup.

There is no account and nothing to log in to. No amount, no customer name and no message content is ever uploaded, shared or sold.

One-time passwords are thrown away

OTPs and security codes are detected and rejected before anything is saved — never stored, never shown in your records, never read aloud. Every notification that is not a payment is discarded immediately.

Bank SMS is optional

The app can receive new bank alerts so card and ATM payments reach your ledger too. Refuse it and the app still works; it simply records fewer payments, and tells you so. It cannot open your message history.

What does leave the phone

Two things, neither containing payment information: crash reports, so a fault can be fixed, and anonymous counts of which screens are opened. Both can be switched off in Settings → Data & privacy, which stops the collection itself.
